06 / Takeaways

Learning Reflection

Throughout the semester, I learned how to design, create, and manage databases using Oracle SQL and Oracle APEX. During the Prelim, I studied conceptual modeling, ERD, normalization, and Supertype/Subtype entities. In the Midterm, I gained hands-on experience in creating and modifying tables, inserting, updating, and deleting records. During the Finals, I practiced using column aliases, WHERE clauses, sorting, and joining tables. These activities helped me understand how databases work and improve my SQL skills. I also learned the importance of accuracy, proper database structure, and applying what I learned in practical activities.

Course Learning Summary

In Database Fundamentals, I learned the basic concepts and practical applications of database management. From Prelim to Finals, I developed skills in database modeling, ERD, normalization, table creation, data manipulation, filtering, sorting, and joining tables using SQL. I also learned how to organize data properly and retrieve information efficiently. Overall, this subject helped strengthen my understanding of databases and prepared me to apply these skills in future IT projects.
